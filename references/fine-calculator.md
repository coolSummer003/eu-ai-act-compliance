# EU AI Act Fine Calculator

## Penalty Structure

The EU AI Act has a tiered penalty system. SMEs and startups benefit from proportionate caps.

### Tier 1: Prohibited AI Practices (Article 5)

- Fine: **€35,000,000 or 7% of global annual turnover**
- Whichever is lower for the entity
- SME/startup cap: lower of the two amounts

### Tier 2: Non-Compliance (Articles 99-101)

- Fine: **€15,000,000 or 3% of global annual turnover**
- Applies to: provider obligations, deployer obligations, transparency violations
- SME/startup cap: lower of the two amounts

### Tier 3: Supply of Incorrect Information (Article 99)

- Fine: **€7,500,000 or 1% of global annual turnover**
- Applies to: misleading information to authorities, false declarations of conformity
- SME/startup cap: lower of the two amounts

## Real Risk Assessment

**The bigger commercial risk is NOT the fine.** It is:

1. **Lost enterprise customers:** Enterprise and public-sector buyers require compliance documentation before signing. No documentation = no deal.

2. **Market access loss:** Products that cannot demonstrate compliance lose access to EU/EEA markets.

3. **Reputational damage:** Non-compliance incidents become public and damage brand trust.

4. **Operational disruption:** Forced product withdrawal, remediation costs, and legal fees.
