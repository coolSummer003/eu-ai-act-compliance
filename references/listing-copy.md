## Product Name

**EU AI Act Compliance Quick Check Skill**

## One-Line Description

Classify your AI systems, map obligations, and generate compliance checklists before the August 2026 deadline.

## English Description

```text
EU AI Act Compliance Quick Check Skill is a workflow package for assessing whether your AI systems fall under the EU AI Act, determining your risk tier, identifying applicable obligations, and generating actionable compliance checklists.

It is designed for AI founders, product managers, compliance officers, and technical teams who need to prepare for the August 2026 deadline without hiring expensive consultants.

Core use cases:
- Classify AI systems by risk tier (prohibited, high-risk, limited, minimal)
- Map obligations based on your role (provider, deployer, importer, distributor)
- Check Article 50 transparency requirements for chatbots and content generation
- Generate compliance checklists with priorities and deadlines
- Conduct gap analysis against existing documentation
- Calculate potential penalties under the EU AI Act

This package includes:
- SKILL.md (complete workflow and classification guide)
- Risk Classifier template (step-by-step system classification)
- Article 50 Transparency Checklist (August 2026 deadline)
- Deployer Obligations template (Article 26 compliance)
- Fine Calculator (penalty estimation and risk assessment)

What this skill does NOT do:
- Does not provide legal advice or guarantee compliance
- Does not replace professional legal counsel for high-risk systems
- Does not generate technical documentation required for conformity assessment
- Does not file registrations with the EU AI database

The EU AI Act applies to any company whose AI systems are placed on the EU market or used in the EU, regardless of where the company is based. Fines reach up to €35M or 7% of global annual turnover. The real risk is losing enterprise customers who require compliance documentation before signing.

This skill focuses on the first step: understanding where you stand and what you need to do next.
```
