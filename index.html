Why This Matters Now

The EU AI Act is the world's first comprehensive AI law. It applies to any company whose AI systems affect EU residents — regardless of where you're based.

February 2025 — Prohibited practices banned Social scoring, manipulative AI, and untargeted facial recognition are now illegal.
August 2025 — GPAI obligations apply Foundation model providers must provide technical documentation and training data summaries.
August 2, 2026 — YOUR DEADLINE Article 50 transparency obligations take effect. High-risk system obligations for Annex III systems.
December 2027 — Full enforcement All high-risk obligations enforceable. National authorities can inspect and fine.

Risk Classification at a Glance

The EU AI Act uses a tiered risk system. The higher the risk, the stricter the obligations.

Risk Tier Examples What's Required
Prohibited Government social scoring, manipulative AI Stop immediately
High-Risk Hiring AI, credit scoring, medical AI Full compliance: risk management, documentation, conformity assessment
Limited Risk Chatbots, AI-generated content, deepfakes Transparency: disclose AI use, label content
Minimal Risk Spam filters, AI games, inventory tools AI literacy only
⚠️ Penalties are real: Up to €35,000,000 or 7% of global annual turnover for prohibited AI. But the bigger risk is losing enterprise customers who require compliance documentation before signing.
